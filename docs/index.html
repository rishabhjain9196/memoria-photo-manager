At a glance

Clean up

Exact duplicates and whole duplicate folders, similar photos and similar videos with an explained best pick, blurry photos, junk files and empty folders — or all at once with Check everything.

Organise

Year/month folders using camera dates, with every move previewed.

People

Recognise faces offline, name people and see every photo of someone; search by name.

Albums

Albums suggested from events, trips, places and people — no files moved — and exported to folders.

Edit

Crop, rotate, mirror, straighten, adjust, resize, split and join photos and videos; trim, speed up or mute clips. Edits are saved as new files — originals never change.

Create

Instagram stories, carousels and story videos; smaller video copies for sharing.

Protect

Health fingerprints, backup comparison and sync, full History with Undo.

Batch

Select many photos and move, copy, compress or send them to the Story maker in one step.

Explore

Folder/type storage charts, year → month → day counts with saved-album links, and an offline India/world GPS map. Browse by size, dates, location and people.

Inspect metadata

Order videos by length, resolution, overall bitrate, FPS and codecs; browse photo ISO, aperture, shutter time, focal length, camera and lens. Missing values are explicit.

Rebuild safely

Safe, custom and hard catalogue reset with restartable phases. Photos, exports, Recovery and Undo history remain; clearing personal catalogue data requires acknowledgement.
